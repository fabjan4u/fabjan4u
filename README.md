## 👨‍🏫 About Me & My Work

I am an ICT educator and teacher-researcher (*docent-onderzoeker*) at **CIV Smart Technology** and **mboRijnland**, based at the **Dutch Innovation Park** in Zoetermeer. 

My journey into technology wasn't traditional: starting as an automotive technician, working in defense, and moving through system and application management before discovering my true passion in education. This hands-on, practical background shapes how I teach and build: **no theoretical fluff, but real hardware, real networks, and real security challenges.**

Today, I focus on bridging the gap between vocational education (MBO), applied sciences (HBO), and the tech industry by creating real-world living labs and open-source tooling:

- 🌉 **OT/ICS Security & "Hack the Bridge":** Guiding students in the **Cyber Security Living Lab (CSyLL)** using physical scale models (e.g., the Botlek bridge with Siemens PLCs) to run authentic Red Team vs. Blue Team scenarios, bridging Information Technology (IT) and Operational Technology (OT).
- 🛡️ **System Hardening & CIS Benchmarks:** Designing and maintaining automated baseline audit tools and modular lab curriculum for Windows Server 2025 and Windows 11 endpoint security.
- 🤖 **AI & Smart Industry Research:** Investigating practical AI applications in education, developing learning pathways for AI literacy, and exploring LLM-assisted security analysis and automated remediation.
- 🤝 **Community & Ecosystem:** Collaborating closely with industry partners (such as Siemens), The Hague University of Applied Sciences (HHS), and regional tech communities to empower the next generation of cybersecurity craftsmen.

## 🚀 Featured Open Source & Educational Projects

### 🔒 [CIS Windows Server 2025 & 11 Hardening Suite](https://github.com/fabjan4u/cis-hardening-suite)
> **Automated CIS Benchmark compliance, baseline auditing, and security reporting engine.**
> Built for systems administrators, educators, and students to audit and harden server environments:
> - Full automated audit against CIS Level 1 and Level 2 baselines.
> - Detailed reporting on password policies, UAC, SMBv1/v2 deprecation, and advanced audit logs.
> - Clear remediation guidance designed to turn complex security baselines into teachable moments.

### 🌉 [OT/ICS Threat & SOC Simulator ("Hack the Bridge")](https://github.com/fabjan4u/hack-the-bridge-soc)
> **Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Blue Team monitoring dashboard and PLC simulation environment.**
> Built as the digital backbone for the *Hack the Bridge* project in the Cyber Security Living Lab:
> - Real-time state telemetry from physical & simulated Siemens PLCs (RUN, STOP, ERROR, Speed, Temp).
> - Live incident intake API for student sensor scripts and defensive alerting.
> - Provides an accessible entry point for students to master Operational Technology security.

### 🕵️‍♂️ [Anonymous WebRTC P2P Chat](https://github.com/fabjan4u/anonieme-chat)
> **A completely anonymous, secure, and lightning-fast Peer-to-Peer chat application.**
> Built with WebRTC, React, and Node.js for zero-log, end-to-end encrypted communication:
> - **100% E2EE & P2P:** Messages flow directly between users via secure DTLS/SCTP data channels.
> - **Zero Metadata & No Logs:** No accounts, no database, and no message logging. Completely temporary sessions.
> - **NAT Traversal:** Implements STUN and TURN servers for seamless communication across strict 4G networks.
> - **Live Demo:** Test the live application at [anonieme-chat.onrender.com](https://anonieme-chat.onrender.com)
